# VNG logo

Official artwork from VNG's own gallery (https://vng.com.vn/gallery/logo.1.html), unmodified:
`MasterArtwork-OrangeVNG Master.png` and `MasterArtwork-WhiteVNG Master.png` — the simplified
wordmark. VNG's brand guidelines (https://vng.com.vn/guidelines/brand/logo.html) apply: use these
files as they are, never stretch, recolour, crop or add effects; full colour (orange on white) when
possible, white on VNG orange or on a dark background otherwise; keep clear space of two V-stroke
widths around it. The VNG logo is a trademark of VNG Corporation.
